Stakeholder Analysis

1. Introduction

The University Library Management System is intended to improve the management of library books, student membership records, borrowing transactions, and book returns.

Stakeholder analysis identifies the people who interact with the system, manage its operations, or are affected by its use. Understanding their needs helps the project team identify appropriate system requirements.

2. Identified Stakeholders

The main stakeholders are:

1. Students
2. Librarians
3. Library Administrators
4. University Management
5. IT Support Staff

3. Stakeholder Analysis Table

Stakeholder	Role	Main Needs	Expected Benefits
Students	Search for books and borrow or return them through library services.	Search books, check availability, and view borrowing status.	Easier access to book information and borrowing records.
Librarians	Manage books, student records, and borrowing transactions.	Register books, issue books, process returns, and track overdue books.	More organized library operations and fewer manual recordkeeping tasks.
Library Administrators	Supervise library activities and monitor records.	Review borrowing activity, monitor overdue books, and access reports.	Better oversight of library operations.
University Management	Oversee university services and resource utilization.	Review library usage and performance information.	Better-informed decisions about library resources.
IT Support Staff	Maintain the technical operation of the system.	Manage technical issues, access permissions, and backups.	Improved system reliability and easier technical maintenance.

4. Stakeholder Needs Summary

Students

Students need a convenient way to search for books and check their availability. They may also need access to their current borrowing status.

Librarians

Librarians need tools to maintain book records, register borrowing transactions, process returns, and identify overdue books.

Library Administrators

Library administrators need to monitor library activities and review relevant records and reports.

University Management

University management may need summarized information about library usage and resource utilization to support planning and decision-making.

IT Support Staff

IT support staff need appropriate access to technical settings, system maintenance functions, and backup procedures.

5. Conclusion

The identified stakeholders have different responsibilities and expectations. Their needs will guide the preparation of the system’s functional and non-functional requirements.

The stakeholder analysis should be reviewed by the project team before the requirements are finalized.
